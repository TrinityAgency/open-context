You do not have to come back to this page to find out whether there is something new. The blog has two feeds, and
every feed reader understands at least one of them.

## Two feeds, one list

The [RSS feed](/feed.xml) and the [Atom feed](/atom.xml) list the same posts, newest first, each with its title, its
date and a short description. Readers follow the link to read the whole post here.

## Following the blog

Copy the address of either feed into your reader. Most readers also find the feeds on their own when you give them
the address of the blog, because every page names them in its head:

```html
<link rel="alternate" type="application/rss+xml" title="RSS" href="/feed.xml" />
<link rel="alternate" type="application/atom+xml" title="Atom" href="/atom.xml" />
```

## What a feed does not do

A feed is a list that readers fetch from this site. It sets no cookie, it tells us nothing about who reads it, and
opening this blog loads nothing from a feed service.
