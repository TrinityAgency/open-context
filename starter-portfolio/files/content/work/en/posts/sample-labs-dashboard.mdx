- **Client:** Sample Labs
- **Our part:** research, design and build of the dashboard; training for the team

## The brief

Sample Labs kept its weekly figures in six spreadsheets. Each Monday someone copied them into a slide, and by
Tuesday the slide was out of date.

## What we did

We sat with the four people who needed the figures and asked what they looked for first. The dashboard shows exactly
that, on one screen, updated every hour from the same spreadsheets.

## The result

The Monday slide is gone. The team now opens the dashboard first thing, and the spreadsheets stay where they were.
