- **Client:** Demo Studio
- **Our part:** the rebuild and a short guide to editing the pages

## The brief

Demo Studio liked how its website looked, but every change took a week and an agency. The pages had also become slow
on a phone.

## What we did

We rebuilt the same pages on a simpler footing: text in plain files, pictures sized for each screen, and nothing
loaded from another host. Then we showed the studio how to change a page in ten minutes.

## The result

The pages load in half the time, and the studio has changed them twenty times since without calling anyone.
