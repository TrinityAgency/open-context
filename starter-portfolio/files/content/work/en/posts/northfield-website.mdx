- **Client:** Northfield Sample
- **Our part:** planning, design, build, and a year of care after the launch
- **Link:** [example.com](https://example.com)

## The brief

Northfield's site had grown to forty pages over ten years. Nobody knew which of them were still read, and the
questions people asked on the phone were answered somewhere on page thirty.

## What we did

We read a year of the questions that came in by email and phone and wrote the site around them: twelve pages, each
answering one of them, each with one heading, one picture and one way onward.

## The result

The site went live eight weeks after the first call. The questions it answers now rarely reach the phone.
