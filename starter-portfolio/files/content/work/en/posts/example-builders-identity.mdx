- **Client:** Example Builders
- **Our part:** the name, the mark, the colours and a two-page guide

## The brief

Three companies had merged into one, and each still used its own name and logo. The new company needed one of each,
and the people in all three had to be able to use them without asking.

## What we did

Four workshops with people from every part of the company led to the name. The mark is a single shape that works in
one colour, at sixteen pixels and on a wall. The guide is two pages long, so that people actually read it.

## The result

Every letter, sign and slide carries the same name and mark six months later, without a brand team to police it.
